# ⚡ Habitron

*A local, analytics-driven habit system that helps you build consistency — intelligently.*

---

### 🧭 Overview

**Habitron** is a desktop application built to help you **build consistency through data**.
It transforms simple habit tracking into a **visual system of insights**, allowing you to understand your progress, identify trends, and stay committed with clarity.
Everything runs **locally** — fast, private, and distraction-free.

---

### ⚙️ Key Features

* 🧩 **Structured Habits:** Add habits with category, difficulty, and importance for deeper analysis.
* 📊 **Visual Insights:** Trends, heatmaps, and correlations — see your consistency evolve over time.
* 💡 **Momentum Index:** A custom metric combining streaks, difficulty, and importance.
* 🧠 **Local & Private:** All data stored securely on your device (SQLite).
* 🔔 **Reminders + Manual Check-ins:** Stay on track your way.
* 🗒️ **Habit Notes:** Reflect and attach quick insights to each habit.

---

### 🧰 Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React + TailwindCSS
* **Backend:** Electron
* **Database:** SQLite (via better-sqlite3)
* **Charts:** Recharts / Chart.js

---

### 🚀 Vision

Habitron isn’t just a tracker — it’s a **consistency engine**.
It helps you *see* your patterns, *measure* your progress, and *build* habits that actually last.
